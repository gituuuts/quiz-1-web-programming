Taste of Mojokerto

Onde-onde is one of the iconic traditional snacks of Mojokerto. It is known for its round shape, sesame-coated exterior, and sweet mung bean filling.

Did you know?

Onde-onde is often associated with celebrations and special occasions in different cultural traditions, including Chinese and local Javanese communities.

A little History

Onde-onde has a long history rooted in Chinese culinary traditions and is believed to have been introduced to Indonesia through Chinese traders. Over time, the recipe adapted to local tastes, with sweet mung bean paste becoming one of its most popular traditional fillings. Today, onde-onde is closely associated with Mojokerto and has become part of the city's culinary identity, often enjoyed as a traditional snack or brought home as a local souvenir.

A legendary taste of Mojokerto since 1929

Looking for an authentic taste of Mojokerto? Onde-Onde Bo Liem is one of the city's legendary traditional snack shops, known for its signature onde-onde since 1929. Its recipes have been passed down through generations, making it a popular destination for those looking for a taste of Mojokerto's culinary heritage
